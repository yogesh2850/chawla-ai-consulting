03 / ROBOTICS DATA PIPELINE

Flagship: data infrastructure for robotics and embodied AI

Robot learning companies collect huge volumes of demonstration data. Before any of it can train a policy, it has to be synchronized, segmented, annotated and packaged. We build the automation layer that turns raw recordings into model-ready episodes.

PIPELINE
  1. Ingest
  2. Sync
  3. Segment
  4. Annotate
  5. QC
  6. Export
  7. Feedback

Outcome: faster turnaround, lower cost per labeled episode, consistent quality across sites, and a pipeline that scales as collection grows.

Inputs
Headset video, multi-camera rigs, IMU, grippers, teleop logs
Sync
Single clock, sub-millisecond target
Segmentation
Episodes, sub-tasks, masks
Annotation
Language, success labels, keypoints
QC
Drift, drops, occlusion checks
Output formats
LeRobot, RLDS, HDF5, Parquet
Delivery
Versioned datasets with data cards

What we automate

  • Ingestion from many sources — in-house rigs, partner factories, customer-supplied logs and teleop sessions.
  • Time alignment and joining — cameras, IMU, hand skeletons, encoders and robot state on one clock.
  • Episode and task segmentation — tasks, sub-tasks and action phases using vision and motion signals.
  • Scene and object segmentation — per-frame masks for hands, tools, objects and workspaces.
  • Annotation — language descriptions, success and failure labels, contact events and keypoints with review queues.
  • Quality control — dropped frames, sync drift, occlusions, calibration errors and incomplete episodes.
  • Packaging and delivery — LeRobot, RLDS and versioned datasets with clear data cards.
  • Feedback from evaluation — model failures linked back so new collection targets the gaps.

CASE STUDY

From days of manual labeling to an automated episode pipeline

Client: a robot-learning data company collecting human and robot demonstrations across several countries.

Challenge

Data arrived from many rigs and partner sites in different formats. Syncing, segmenting and labeling each episode took days of manual work, and quality varied by site.

What we built

An automated pipeline for ingestion, clock alignment, task segmentation, hand and object masks, language annotation and QC, with export to the client's training formats and a review tool for low-confidence cases.

07 / FAQ

Before you ask

What kinds of companies do you work with?

Teams that need AI in production — robotics and embodied AI companies, manufacturers, agtech, healthcare, financial services, and retail and logistics operators. Early startups through established enterprises.

Can you work with our existing tools and cloud?

Yes. We integrate with AWS, GCP and Azure, and with your existing labeling tools, object stores, ROS stacks and training formats. We adapt to your stack rather than forcing a rewrite.

How do you price projects?

Fixed-scope projects are quoted against deliverables and timeline. Dedicated teams and managed pipelines are monthly. Advisory is day-rate or retainer. Discovery calls are free.

Who owns the code and models you build?

You do. Unless we agree otherwise in writing, you receive full IP transfer for custom code, models and documentation at handoff.

How do you keep our data secure?

NDAs by default, role-based access, encrypted storage and transfer, audit-ready logs, and data handling aligned to your compliance needs. Your data is never used to train our own models without consent.

Do you only work on robotics?

No. Robotics data infrastructure is our flagship practice, but we deliver AI strategy, data pipelines, annotation, computer vision, LLM automation and MLOps across industries.

Have data piling up and no time to process it?

Tell us what you collect and what you want out of it. We will reply within two business days with an approach and a rough estimate.
